Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Adhan & Qibla does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services of any kind built into the app.

Location

If you grant location access, it is used only on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction for where you are. Your location is never sent anywhere — not to the developer, not to any server, not to any third party. It is not stored beyond what the app needs to show you current times, and you can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings.

Data storage

Any places you save, your settings, and your prayer-tracking history are stored only on your device (and, if you use iCloud, synced privately through your own iCloud account exactly like Apple's built-in apps do). None of it is visible to the developer.

Notifications

Prayer-time alerts are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device using iOS's local notification system. No push notification service or external server is involved.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page.
