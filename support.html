Support

Adhan & Qibla shows accurate prayer times, adhan playback, and Qibla direction — fully offline, with no account and nothing collected. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Questions or problems?

Email 65-cuties.haggis@icloud.com and describe what happened, including your device model and iOS version if it's a bug. Screenshots help.

Common questions

Prayer times look off for my location.
Open the app, go to Places, and check that the right city (or your current GPS location) is set as active. You can also adjust the calculation angles under a location's settings if your local mosque uses a different method.

I'm not getting adhan notifications.
Make sure notifications are enabled for Adhan & Qibla in iOS Settings, and that alerts are turned on for each prayer inside the app's Alerts screen.

Does this app work without internet?
Yes. Prayer times and Qibla direction are calculated entirely on your device.
